◐Zoom

Bring your thumb and index finger together on one hand, then move them apart. The gap between your fingertips maps directly to zoom level.

↔Change slide

Open your whole hand, fingers spread, and sweep it left or right. A short pause between sweeps keeps it from double-triggering.

↕Scroll

Point with just your index and middle fingers, other fingers curled, and move your hand up or down to scroll the caption text.

Hand tracking runs entirely in your browser using MediaPipe's on-device model — the video frame never leaves your machine. If the camera preview stays black, check your browser's site permissions and allow camera access, and make sure no other app is using the webcam. Works best in good, even lighting with your hand roughly arm's length from the camera.
